# Design — SIMI 3000

The design intent: what the site should be and why. Exact values (sizes, timings, colours) live
in the code. Decisions here are Tobias's — if something isn't covered, ask; don't invent.

## The page

One screen, no scrolling, on phone and desktop. On a single centred axis: **SIMI 3000** in Arial bold at
the top, the photo as large as fits, then two links, **E-Mail** and **Instagram**. Small
Impressum and Datenschutz links at the very bottom. All links are classic, unstyled blue HTML
links. German only; no other text.

## The photo

Simi in a cactus garden, seen through **frosted glass**: soft, milky, a little grainy. Colour
and shape come through, detail doesn't.

- **Wiping it clear:** where the pointer (or a finger) moves, the sharp photo shows through,
  leaving a short trail that frosts over again.
- **Inviting touch (desktop):** until the visitor moves over the photo, the frost slowly
  breathes, thicker and thinner, so it's clearly alive. It stops on the first movement and
  returns after the pointer has been still for a while.
- **Reduced motion:** the photo stays frosted; nothing reveals or moves.

## The cursor (desktop)

Tobias's white cartoon glove, pointing up, replaces the pointer everywhere. Over the photo and
over anything clickable it tilts clearly counter-clockwise around the fingertip, and
straightens when it leaves. Phones keep normal touch.
